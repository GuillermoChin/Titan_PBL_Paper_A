# Figures and tables of the revised manuscript

Each row gives the item of the revised manuscript, the section where it appears,
the released file, the name used inside the code, the script that draws or
writes it, and the analysis outputs it reads. Paths under `build/` are created
by `code/run_all.py`. "Framework" means the subset in `code/titan_pbl_subset/`
evaluated on the HASI profile, with no time integration; "archive" means the
published output of Hartwick et al. (2026, doi:10.5281/zenodo.17246644); "TAM"
means that of Han and Lora (2025, doi:10.5281/zenodo.16791001). The numbers
drawn or printed come from `numbers/numbers_paper_A.json`; the keys used by each
figure are listed in `figures/figures_manifest.csv`.
